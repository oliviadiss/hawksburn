Estate & Legacy Planning

We begin by understanding what a family wants to protect, preserve and pass on, before turning to structures or investments.

Without clarity about intent, control and succession, even strong investment performance can miss the outcome a family actually wanted. We remain the constant reference point across the process.

  • Trusts and foundations
  • Wills and guardianship
  • Protector and council services
  • Family constitutions
  • Succession planning
  • Cross-border estate coordination

Estate intent informs structure.

Structuring & Corporate Architecture

Structure is not administrative. It determines how effectively wealth can be preserved, moved and deployed.

We design bespoke holding architectures in which every entity has a defined purpose, governance role and reporting line. Jurisdiction selection is driven by substance rather than fashion, and structures are updated as laws and family circumstances change.

  • Holding companies and SPVs
  • Jurisdiction selection
  • Incorporation and registered offices
  • Corporate secretarial
  • Accounting, FATCA and CRS
  • Banking architecture

Structure determines the investment vehicles available.

Family Office Setup & Management

Whether a single, multi or hybrid family office is right depends on the family, not on a template.

Families receive a functioning operating platform, not just an approval. We assess eligibility for the 13O, 13U and 13D incentives, coordinate applications and ongoing compliance, and once the office exists, we run it.

  • SFO, MFO or hybrid assessment
  • 13O, 13U and 13D applications
  • Staffing, payroll and controls
  • EP and DP applications
  • Property and lifestyle
  • Healthcare and mobility

The office runs the structure day to day.

Investment Vehicles (VCC Platform)

An institutional-grade Variable Capital Company platform, with segregated sub-funds that can serve a single family or a single purpose.

Hawksburn Capital acts as fund manager to our umbrella VCC, alongside an independent fund administrator and board of directors. Sub-funds allow families to invest with privacy without giving up control.

  • Umbrella VCC structures
  • Bespoke family sub-funds
  • Public and private assets
  • Co-investments
  • Investment committee oversight
  • Administrator and auditor coordination

Vehicles shape portfolio construction and reporting.

Our story

From 2005, our founders worked at one of Australia’s largest single family offices, serving a multi-billion-dollar family from Hawksburn Village, Melbourne. One office, one client, and no distance between the advice and its consequences. The firm takes its name from that place.

It taught them what a family really wants from an office of its own: someone who holds the whole picture, who is there when something goes wrong, and who thinks in decades rather than quarters.

Hawksburn was founded in Singapore in 2011 to offer that same discipline to families who would rather not build an office of their own.

  1. 2005Trained inside a single family officeOur founders train at one of Australia’s largest single family offices in Melbourne, learning the work in practice.
  2. 2011Hawksburn is established in SingaporeThe same approach, offered to families across Asia and beyond.
  3. TodayAn integrated platformEstate planning, structuring, family office management, our own VCC platform, investment management and consolidated reporting, under one relationship.
Commissioned photograph of the new office

What we took from it

Discipline over fashion. Jurisdictions chosen for substance. Structures reviewed rather than left to drift. Reporting that shows the whole position, including the parts that are inconvenient.

And a preference for long relationships over transactions. Most of what we do only proves its worth years after the decision was taken.
